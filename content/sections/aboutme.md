I am an Associate Professor at the [University of Oregon](https://education.uoregon.edu/), where I also hold the Petrone Professorship.

Educational equity is at the heart of my research. I care deeply about students' civil rights in schools, and my work follows three connected strands. First, I use demographic techniques to examine and highlight key disparities in students' learning opportunities. Second, I use experimental and quasi-experimental research designs to inform policies and programs that aim to reduce those disparities. Third, I study for whom, and in what contexts, policies at the intersection of health and education are effective. Across all three, I draw on interdisciplinary social science training that spans economics, public policy, and social psychology to inform the design and evaluation of evidence-based education and social policies in the US.

Together with Erica Frankenberg, I lead the [AdvancED Equity Initiative](https://advancedequity.org). In 2023–24, I completed a public service tour as the inaugural Data Science Impact Fellow at the Office for Civil Rights in the U.S. Department of Education. Previously, I was a [Social Science Research Institute](https://ssri.psu.edu/) co-funded Faculty Fellow and an Associate Professor of Education and Public Policy at [The Pennsylvania State University](https://www.psu.edu/), and an Associate Editor at [*Educational Researcher*](https://journals.sagepub.com/home/edr). Currently, I serve on the Board of Directors of the [Association for Education Finance and Policy (AEFP)](https://aefpweb.org/current_bod) and the [Institute for Replication (I4R)](https://www.i4replication.org/about).
